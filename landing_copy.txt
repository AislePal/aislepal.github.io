FAQ
Eyebrow: FAQ
Headline: Questions, honestly answered.
Q: How fast do retailers see basket lift?
A: Most pilots see measurable basket-size lift within the first 30 days, driven by easier product discovery and reduced abandonment. The biggest gains arrive in months 2–3 as recommendations and promotions tune to live behavioral data.
Q: Who owns the data — us or AislePal?
A: You do. Always. AislePal is the infrastructure that captures and structures your in-store intent data, but every byte belongs to your business. Full export at any time, no lock-in.
Q: How does this comply with CCPA and GDPR?
A: AislePal is privacy-first by design. No persistent shopper IDs unless explicitly opted in via your loyalty program. Anonymous session-based capture by default. Data residency, retention windows, and deletion are all configurable per region.
Q: What happens if a shopper doesn't want to scan the QR?
A: The store works exactly as it does today. AislePal is purely additive — never required, never gated. Shoppers who scan get a better trip; everyone else shops normally. There's no downside to non-adoption.
Q: Do we need new hardware?
A: No. AislePal runs entirely on shoppers' phones via a web app. The only physical change is QR codes at entrances and key shelf locations — printed, mounted, done.
Q: How does this fit alongside our existing loyalty program?
A: It enhances it. Loyalty programs tell you who bought what. AislePal tells you what they searched, considered, and skipped. Linked together, they become the most complete picture of an in-store shopper any retailer has ever had.
